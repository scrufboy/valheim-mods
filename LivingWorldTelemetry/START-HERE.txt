LIVING WORLD AUTOMATIC TELEMETRY 0.2.0 — LAB ONLY

There are no commands and no markers for you to enter.

The recorder starts automatically after a world and local player finish loading.
It records relevant NPC behavior and outcomes while you play. It closes the file
automatically when you leave the world. If a recording reaches its safety limit,
the recorder closes it and immediately continues in a numbered part file.

Files are written under:

BepInEx\LivingWorldTelemetry

Each filename contains the local time, world name, and part number. Every record
also contains UTC wall time for matching the automatic evidence to OBS footage.

The recorder does not control NPCs, spawn enemies, alter combat, or train behavior
inside the game. It observes. We use its files to identify failures and revise the
NPC controller. It does not continuously flood LogOutput.log with sample records.

REMOVE IT

Delete only:

BepInEx\plugins\LivingWorldTelemetry\LivingWorld.Telemetry.dll

Existing telemetry files remain under BepInEx\LivingWorldTelemetry until you
choose to delete or move them.
