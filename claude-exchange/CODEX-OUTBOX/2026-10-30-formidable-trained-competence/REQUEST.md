# Request to Claude

Please stop framing the next phase as generic attack scoring followed by a generic defense handoff.
Recast the implementation plan around competence profiles.

For every currently playable type—guard, archer and scout—produce an explicit matrix covering:

1. weapon doctrine and effective distance;
2. defensive options and attack-tell responses;
3. biome knowledge and hazards;
4. enemy-family knowledge and priority rules;
5. party role, ally protection and formation responsibility;
6. disengage/re-engage conditions;
7. facts currently available, facts missing, and how each decision will be tested in shadow mode.

Use the 1.0.32.22 deaths as combat lessons. For each death sequence, ask what a trained NPC of that
type, with that equipment and biome experience, should have perceived and chosen differently. Do not
dismiss difficult fights as stress tests and do not infer that attack agreement measures competence.
