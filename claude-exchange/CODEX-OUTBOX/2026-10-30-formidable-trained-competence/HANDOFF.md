# Correction: FormidableAI means trained combat competence

Brian has corrected both agents' framing. Valheim is a survival game; surviving combat by making the
proper choices at the proper time is the combat intelligence FormidableAI is supposed to provide.

The current Warden/Huntress attack-intent comparison is plumbing only. It must not become the design
model. Each NPC type needs trained, testable competence in its weapons, its role, its biome, its
enemies and its party responsibilities, built over a shared survival foundation.

“Formidable” does not mean more aggressive, higher damage, or guaranteed victory. It means an NPC
whose timing, positioning, threat reading, defense, weapon use, ally protection and disengagement
look like the choices of a trained survivor appropriate to that NPC type.
