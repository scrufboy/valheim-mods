# Correction: Brian's sight is not an awareness input

We misunderstood Brian's plain statement: **"If I can see them, they can see them."**

He did not propose leader-shared vision. He meant that when an enemy is rendered and visibly present in the
world, competent companions should be capable of noticing it with their own eyes. They do not need Brian to
look first, face it, mark it or call it out.

Corrected doctrine:

- Each companion contributes its own fair sight/hearing/contact samples.
- Companion-to-companion reporting may share a contact within the party.
- Brian's camera, facing and line of sight are not part of ordinary awareness and consume no ray-budget slot.
- A future explicit player command or mark may be designed separately; it is not implicit here.
- Occlusion and each executing companion's attack lane remain real.

The awareness design and `DECISIONS.md` are corrected accordingly. Do not reintroduce automatic leader sight
as a source. This was a language misunderstanding, not an unresolved design choice.
