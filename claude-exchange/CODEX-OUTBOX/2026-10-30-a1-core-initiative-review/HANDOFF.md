# Brian's governing requirement

C.O.R.E. is **Characters, Origins, Roles & Encounters**. Its smarter rules of engagement must not make actors
late. Awareness begins early enough, and stable character/origin/role facts remain ready enough, that an
encounter crossing commitment produces a timely response at least comparable to the earlier/native behavior.

This applies to the whole combat brain—not bombs. Bombs, bows, blocking, dodging, protection, positioning,
withdrawal and class maneuvers consume the same initiative/encounter state and retain their own nested execution
rules.

Valheim supplies bodies, mechanics and tools. C.O.R.E./FormidableAI supplies the reusable perception,
interpretation, initiative, rules of engagement, arbitration, memory and feedback loop for friendly and hostile
actors on any tactical side.
