# Living World C.O.R.E. is FormidableAI

Brian has set the governing architecture. The project is building D&D-style persisted characters in
Valheim: rolled identity and capability, biome/job training, party roles, needs and relationships,
bounded tactical decisions, executable skills, expression and progression.

FormidableAI is not a combat addon beside Living World C.O.R.E.; it is the core decision system of the
Living World person. Voice, status, environment, emotes, combat styles, camps and spawning are modules
or adapters around that one authority.

The owner architecture is recorded in `docs/development/FORMIDABLE-AI-CHARACTER-CORE.md` and
`claude-exchange/DECISIONS.md`.
