# Character rollup, training and loadout are one system

Brian has defined the missing owner boundary: training is part of the weapon loadout and character
rollup. FormidableAI must consume a persisted character profile, not guess Warden/Huntress from the
current guard/archer prefab.

The starting profile combines origin biome, people/culture, gender expression, job/type, individual
variance, stats, skills, armor, food budget, weapons, ammunition, utility equipment and the doctrine
those facts authorize. World progression scales the starting package. Later skill and equipment
changes update capability without rerolling identity.

Quality-of-life rules must be explicit invariants rather than accidents. Brian's example is endless
utility torches: companions should simply remain capable of routine lighting without player
inventory maintenance. Combat ammunition and special consumables remain separately balanced.
