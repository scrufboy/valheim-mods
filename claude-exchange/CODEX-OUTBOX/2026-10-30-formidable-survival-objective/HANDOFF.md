# FormidableAI survival objective

Brian corrected the framing after the 1.0.32.22 wipe: every NPC must always try to beat its
opponents. Survival and winning are core AI objectives even before leveling and equipment
progression exist.

The ten deaths are therefore not dismissed as an intentional overload result. Tier matters for
diagnosis—Seekers were higher tier, while the Plains-quality pairs should have been more evenly
matched against Fulings—but FormidableAI should still make the best survivable decision available.

This does not authorize invulnerability or stat cheating. It makes defense, threat response,
spacing, ally protection, disengagement and action timing part of the replacement brain's minimum
competence.
