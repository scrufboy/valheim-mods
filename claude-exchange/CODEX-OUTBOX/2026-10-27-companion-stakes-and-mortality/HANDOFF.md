# Companion stakes, limits, and permanent mortality

## Owner correction

The current pause/console party creation is a test shortcut. It is not recruitment
and would be cheating as a campaign feature. Formidable companions require real
discovery, investment, limits, upkeep, risk, and permanent loss.

This handoff extends the appearance/progression/roster handoff without changing
its established names, biome roles, or two-person active field limit.

## Binding design

- two active expedition companions;
- six living bonded companions total by default, including the active two;
- ordinary known people and residents are not owned combat reserves;
- legitimate recruits originate only from persistent world encounters;
- debug-spawned actors are permanently ineligible for campaign promotion;
- one unresolved recruitment lead at a time;
- five in-game days before another lead becomes eligible after recruitment,
  refusal, abandonment, or a companion death;
- eligibility begins a new search in an unexplored valid area; it never spawns a
  replacement beside the player;
- recruitment requires encounter resolution, a valid home/bed, and an open bonded
  seat; continued service consumes real food, ammunition, repairs, gear, and risk;
- Release from Party at a safe home/camp frees a living bond without deleting the
  person;
- actual companion death is permanent. There is no Third Breath, bed respawn,
  recall resurrection, cleric resurrection, or reload repair;
- equipment may be recovered through one real corpse/tombstone transaction and a
  persistent memorial retains the person's history;
- a future wounded window may occur only before committed death and must end in
  permanent death if rescue fails.

## Safety gate

Do not enable permanent mortality until traversal, water/lava response, network
ownership, duplicate death suppression, and authoritative cause attribution are
live-proven. This gate protects players from losing a person to a known movement
or replication defect; it does not weaken the final mortality rule.

## Existing test code

Do not remove or repurpose the current spawn tools. Mark and isolate their output
from campaign records. Current combat/traversal stabilization remains untouched
until its own acceptance work is complete.
