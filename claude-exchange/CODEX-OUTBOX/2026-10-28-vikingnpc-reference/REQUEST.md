# Owner direction

The owner supplied `RustyMods-VikingNPC-0.3.4.zip` and an Atlas video transcript.
He sees major overlap with the living-world vision and wants the non-relationship
features examined for full realization in Living World C.O.R.E. His own
help/consent/affinity/alliance system remains unique. Specifically carry forward
biome people and gear, independent work and survival, inventory persistence,
contextual voices and emotes, ship travel, live structure routines, and combat
that respects each weapon's actual moves and resources.

The owner says every taming or hiring module is the wrong relationship model
for people in this world. Work, survival, equipment, combat, conversation, and
travel capabilities must connect to our existing person/encounter records
without creating a taming meter, hiring contract, payment-to-follow command,
or pet-style ownership state.

The owner's latest live review also says that some pushing remains; no-push is
an open acceptance gate. Deliberate slow walking exposes NPC stop/surge jitter,
though he considers that lower priority. Use the related 1.0.32.8 handoff.
