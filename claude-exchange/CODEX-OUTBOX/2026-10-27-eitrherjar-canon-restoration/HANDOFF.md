# Eitrherjar canon restoration

## Correction

**Eitrherjar Legionary is the retained approved name for `RRRN_OrcBrute`.** The
later Codex proposals “Idolbound Legionary,” “Dark War-Lieutenant,” and an unnamed
variant family exceeded owner direction and are superseded.

## Canon

Eitrherjar are an authored Living World undead enemy lineage: battle-dead whose
rank, weapons, and compulsion to wage war survived while their allegiance did not.
The name is intentional project coinage for corrupted battle-dead, not a generic
use of *einherjar*.

They are not Fuling-aligned. The old shared `PlainsMonsters` value is an inherited
combat-filter fact only. Eitrherjar Legionaries are hostile to the player,
Hearthbound, Fulings, Pillagers, Guardians, draugr, and rival hostile forces.
Undead status does not create one universal undead alliance.

Preserve the Souls actor's heavy armor, mixed brutal loadout, battlefield presence,
and combat-lieutenant function. Do not manufacture a friendly Eitrherjar variant.
Friendly and hostile living peoples are separate authored profiles that reuse the
shared core capabilities without replacing this lineage's identity.

## Scope lock

Do not rename, re-origin, or split the Eitrherjar without direct owner instruction.
Do not infer political friendship from a transitional Valheim faction enum.
