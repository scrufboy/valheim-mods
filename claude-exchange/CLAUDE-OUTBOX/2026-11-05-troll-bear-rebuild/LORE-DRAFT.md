# The Sunken Folk — lore draft (for Brian's approval)

Drafted by Claude from Brian's statements only. Words in [brackets] are open for Brian to fill or change.

---

Before the rot, the swamps were not swamps. They were lush jungle, warm and green, home to another people — builders in stone whose great figures still stand half-drowned among the reeds. [Inca-pack ruins.]

Then the men of the north came, and they did not share. They named the jungle-dwellers *swamp folk*, cut them off, and left them to the water and everything that hunts in it. The jungle drowned. The stone castles cracked and sank. The dead began to walk.

The swamp folk did not die. They changed.

**The little Necks** still hunt as they always have — Leeches in the upper swamp, quick and quiet. They want nothing from the dead. Leave them be and they leave you be; a draugr that lays a hand on them learns otherwise.

**The Sunken Marauders** went below. Down by the crypts they dig — always digging, picks ringing on stone for iron that never comes. Work without reward is all the swamp left them. They hunt Leeches when they surface, and they bubble up out of the mud when something disturbs the digging. Some fight with the picks they carry. Others took up the spears and clubs of the Fulings. None of them forgot who drove them here.

**The Neck Shaman** wears crimson. Where the Fuling shamans speak for their warbands, the Neck Shaman speaks for the swamp folk — and the swamp folk listen. It speaks to something else as well.

**The Colossus** walked out of the old jungle's dead. It wanders the broken castles and the crypt-mouths, and it hears. A sound from far off and it comes. It belongs to the zombies — until a Neck Shaman calls it. Then it fights for the swamp folk against everything and everyone, and when at last it falls it bursts into the thickest poison cloud the swamp has ever breathed.

Humans do not belong here. The swamp remembers why.
