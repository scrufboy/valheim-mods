# Living World FreeCam — controller controls

Installed profile: **LWC Human Lab v1**

## Main controls

- **Click both sticks together (L3 + R3):** enter or exit free camera
- **Left stick:** fly forward/back/sideways
- **Right stick:** look around
- **Right trigger / left trigger:** rise / descend
- **Left bumper / right bumper:** zoom / widen field of view

## Menu-button combinations

Hold the controller's **Menu/Start** button, then press:

- **A:** hide/show HUD
- **B:** hide/show help panels
- **X:** save PNG screenshot
- **Y:** cycle camera flight speed (2, 5, 10, 20, 40)
- **LB / RB:** tilt camera left/right in 5-degree steps
- **LB + RB:** level the camera
