# Rimebound and Emberworn approval checklist

Use a disposable test world, spawn one adult at a time, and record the matching
startup log. Natural regional spawns remain disabled until this page passes.

- [ ] Registration: both exact prefab IDs register once; originals still register.
- [ ] Silhouette: adult humanlike regional people; neither reads as a child.
- [ ] Northern clothing: wolf layers read as warm/fur-lined and do not clip badly.
- [ ] Northern antlers: horned helm gives a clearly larger crown silhouette.
- [ ] Northern palette/glow: winter-white and blue-white effect, readable but not blinding.
- [ ] Southern clothing: ragged/flowing heat-region silhouette, not heavy winter armor.
- [ ] Southern headpiece: dragon headdress reads as a southern regional adaptation.
- [ ] Southern palette/glow: ember/red-orange effect, readable but not constant screen noise.
- [ ] Idle/movement: no floating, foot sliding, animation lock or equipment conflict.
- [ ] Normal combat: axe cadence, pathing and ordinary damage remain believable.
- [ ] Northern special: clear windup, visible frost projectile, bounded impact and cooldown.
- [ ] Southern special: clear windup, visible fire projectile, bounded impact and cooldown.
- [ ] Faction safety: both adults attack intended player/hostile targets, not same-faction allies.
- [ ] Lore overlay: the three mapped prefabs log `verified=True`; the three excluded prefabs retain their names.
- [ ] Lore isolation: disabling `General.Enabled` restores original display names after restart without editing Souls JSON.
- [ ] Social boundary: neither approval enemy exposes tame/command behavior.
- [ ] Performance: no repeated-effect spam, runaway projectiles, log flood or material leak.
- [ ] Death/drop: effects and drops occur once and contain no missing-prefab errors.

Approval requires live evidence. Static validation and successful registration are
not visual or gameplay approval.
