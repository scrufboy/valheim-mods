# World companion discovery and biome-role architecture

Status: design authority for wilderness recruitment, biome class pairs, and
reclaimed-village population flow.

## Core fiction

Friendly people already live, travel, hunt, fail, and survive in the world before
the player reaches them. They are not generated by a shop and do not wait in an
empty field for an interaction prompt. A recruitable person appears as part of a
small encounter with a campsite, task, route, danger, allegiance, and reason to
move on.

A reclaimed village is one destination for survivors, not their origin. The same
stable person first met at a field camp can later become a companion, guard,
resident, specialist, or village leader without being replaced by another NPC.

## Wilderness encounter grammar

Every encounter is composed from six records:

1. **Origin** — regional people, exile, survivor, expedition, mercenary band,
   reclaimed-village scout, or faction defector.
2. **Site** — temporary camp, hunting blind, ruined tower, cooking fire, road
   watch, resource claim, wounded retreat, burial site, or besieged shelter.
3. **Activity** — cook, eat, sleep, sit, sharpen, repair, forage, hunt, patrol,
   practice, tend wounds, study, mourn, argue, or watch an enemy route.
4. **Relationship** — neutral, wary, friendly, indebted, allied, hostile, or
   tolerant of a local enemy faction.
5. **Pressure** — nearby creatures, raid, dwindling supplies, injured member,
   rival people, lost route, cursed site, or no immediate crisis.
6. **Outcome** — remain, trade, ask aid, offer trial, join temporarily, accept a
   village invitation, become recruitable, depart, or become hostile.

Sites must use a bounded footprint and safe placement rules: reachable ground,
biome-valid terrain, distance from player structures and boss/sacrificial areas,
no lava or deep water trap, and no overlap with another persistent encounter.

## What the player sees

The first read is animation and behavior, not text. At least two members of a
field group should perform staggered activities while another watches. Equipment
is stowed unless danger or work requires it. The group may cook, rest, repair,
spar, hunt local wildlife, or actively fight a faction it opposes.

Local faction tolerance must be authored rather than inferred from a transitional
Valheim faction enum. The Souls actor used for the **Eitrherjar Legionary**
currently ignores Fulings because both use `PlainsMonsters`; that is implementation
history, not a Living World alliance. The Eitrherjar relationship is hostile to
players, Hearthbound camps, Fulings, draugr, and other combatants unless a specific
encounter creates a short-lived tactical exception. That exception never rewrites
the lineage's allegiance.

## Recruitment context and answer

Recruitment has no minigame or random persuasion roll. Shared defense, rescue,
proof, provision, standing, sanctuary, and compatible purpose establish facts the
person can know. The player eventually asks: **Will you come with me?** The answer
is yes or no, deterministically supported by those facts and the person's current
obligations. Personality changes expression, not the truth of the answer.

No path instantly erases the person's companions or camp. Non-recruited members
may remain, relocate, visit the reclaimed village later, or supply another
encounter. Declining a recruit is not a failure state.

[The Second Breath](./LORE-BIBLE-THE-SECOND-BREATH.md) and

## Class, loadout, and person

Sex, body, voice, hair, and origin are identity variants, never combat classes.
A class is separate only when it changes range, timing, movement, resource use,
target preference, or battlefield responsibility.

The baseline **Scout** has two role preferences over one shared loadout:

- **Bow-first Scout:** opens at range and switches to sword/shield when closed.
- **Close-first Scout:** opens with sword/shield and uses the bow for unreachable,
  fleeing, or safely distant targets.

They are not two classes. Both remain adaptable Valheim scouts.

## Biome class pairs

The pair is a combat relationship, not a mandatory male/female pairing. Each role
may have multiple identity presentations. Equipment tier follows world progress,
but a recruit's survival package must be credible for the biome where found.

| Biome | Mobile/precision role | Pressure/control role | Encounter read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meadows | Scout, bow-first or close-first | Shield hand / woodsman | hunters, cooking camp, road watch |
| Black Forest | Knife skirmisher / forest scout | Stocky axe breaker | ruin crew, charcoal camp, greydwarf hunt |
| Swamp | Spear warden / draugr hunter | Mace-and-shield breaker | raised camp, crypt watch, medicine run |
| Mountains | Javelin outrider / frost scout | Two-handed axe vanguard | fire shelter, silver route, wolf watch |
| Plains | Black-metal spear hunter | Heavy club/atgeir/axe reaver | Fuling counter-hunt, farm watch, siege remnant |
| Mistlands | Knife/seeker hunter or crossbow scout | Rune warder / staff support | mist beacon, excavation, stranded expedition |
| Ashlands | Veteran mobile hunter upgraded for heat | Veteran siege vanguard / sapper | established survivors only; no casual novice camp |
| Deep North | Cold outrider | Rime axe guardian / rune support | established survivors adapted for lethal cold |

The Plains pair is the next new spawnable encounter after the reusable bomb
capability: a fast spear hunter and a heavy pressure fighter. Together they mirror
the problem posed by Fulings without copying Fuling identity. The spear hunter
throws and closes; the heavy actor staggers, bashes, and controls close space.

## Raid response as character revelation

When a raid begins, ambient activity cancels through the exclusive action-owner
contract. The response is staged and role-specific:

1. alarm perception and vocal call;
2. stand/turn, release work-hand lease, and stop social emote;
3. retrieve prepared equipment through native hand/back lifecycle;
4. class-specific assembly position around the defended anchor;
5. native combat acquisition and execution;
6. bounded special actions only while combat owns the actor;
7. verified clear, regroup, casualty check, then delayed return to camp activity.

The spear hunter seeks a clear throwing lane; the heavy fighter protects the camp
entrance or closes on a stagger opportunity; scouts cover flanks; support magic
holds behind the pressure line. No peaceful camp controller may continue moving,
facing, or animating an actor after combat takes ownership.

## Persistence and population

Each authored person receives a stable identity before the player can recruit
them. Persist origin, encounter, faction, role, equipment record, injuries,
relationship, recruitment state, home, and current assignment. Store encounter
resolution separately so unloaded camps can be retired without deleting recruited
people.

Population budgets limit simultaneous sites per biome and world region. Rejected
or unresolved camps expire or migrate after a lore-appropriate interval. Recruits
and named survivors never expire through the ambient population budget.
