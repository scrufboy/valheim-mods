Building-role registry foundation (not installed; no native patches)

BuildingRegistry stores immutable settlement/building/marker identities and locations.
Roles: Kitchen, Smithy, Workshop, Barracks, Healing, GreatHall, Housing.
Every building and marker has one settlement owner. Invalid reassignment is rejected
without losing the old assignment. Removing markers only removes role declarations;
forgetting a building removes its marker assignments, never native pieces.
Lookup considers role, explicit distance, settlement bounds, and deterministic ties.

Native adapter plan:
1. Owner-authoritative game-thread adapter derives settlement identity from maypole
   ZDOID and supplies actual bounds including linked outposts. Never infer ownership
   solely from nearest center where areas overlap.
2. Survey loaded pieces into explicit building records. Building grouping is a
   separate problem: this registry does not claim native prefabs are blueprints.
3. Persist marker identity, settlement identity, building identity, role and position
   in versioned ZDO fields; reconstruct registry after load, without duplicating
   native geometry. Handle explicit marker role changes and native destruction.
4. Steward looks up facilities and queues work. Registry does not teleport villagers,
   grant items, fabricate food, activate production, or assign controllers itself.
5. Interaction can use normal game/controller input. Do not add global input hooks.

Visual options require native asset inspection and gameplay testing:
- Private clone of the native piece_guardstone visual, stripping ward/private-area
  components ONLY from our clone; retain separately defined networking/placement.
  Never change the global original prefab or claim ward coverage is our boundary.
- Private colored shield marker on wall, or a small pedestal/item-holder decoration.
  Per-role material clones prevent recoloring original assets.
No visual prefab registration or compatibility guarantee is supplied here.

This foundation does not record blueprints, rebuild missing pieces, demolish a
building, establish NPC ownership of beds, or replace existing gameplay behavior.
