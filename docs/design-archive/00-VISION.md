# Vision

Living World C.O.R.E. is a modular character, origin, role, and encounter system
for persistent, player-like people in Valheim. It is intended to replace the
project's dependency on RRRCore through independently implemented contracts in the
Formidable NPC Framework, while keeping Living World lore and content separate.

The core actor is one person, not a collection of disposable role prefabs. The
same identity may be discovered in the world, become known, join the bonded roster,
serve in the two-person expedition party, guard, work, reside in a reclaimed town,
or return home. Role changes preserve identity, history, health, equipment,
relationships, and appearance.

Valheim is primarily traversal punctuated by danger. A formidable NPC must therefore
be convincing while following, regrouping, avoiding people, crossing doors and
terrain, entering portals and dungeons, fighting, recovering, holding, guarding,
working, resting, and persisting through unloads and restarts.

The framework is reusable by community creators. Living World content consumes it
without making the Tooler, telemetry, private compatibility repairs, RRRCore,
Souls of the Land, or licensed asset packs permanent framework dependencies.

Single-player quality is the primary experience, but every authoritative state and
transition must remain correct for remote clients and dedicated servers.
