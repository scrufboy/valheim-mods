# Competitive bar

Offline Companions demonstrates useful needs, food/mead use, home modes, jobs,
equipment upkeep and persistent state. Vikings for Hire demonstrates world-found
people, bounded squads, roster progression, relationships and companion-to-worker
role changes.

These are methodology evidence, not source code or behavior to copy blindly.

LWC must exceed both by combining:

- one authoritative persistent person across expedition and settlement life;
- native-combat ownership with measurable formidability and preserved player agency;
- believable traversal, spacing, doors, portals and instanced interiors;
- meaningful discovery, investment, upkeep, release, home and permanent loss;
- modular framework contracts usable by community content;
- explicit multiplayer authority, provenance, migrations and regression gates;
- living-world roles and factions that do not reduce people to purchased summons.

Competitor respawn/revive mechanics are explicitly rejected after committed death.
Useful architecture may be learned; licensed assets and implementation are not taken.
