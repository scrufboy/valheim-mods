# Combat roadmap

`Formidable` means credible player-like combat contribution, not invulnerability
or autonomous victory. Two correctly equipped companions must not reliably defeat
a same-tier boss or full raid while the player remains passive.

Preserve native stamina, ammunition, durability, recovery, attack commitment,
line of fire, interruption, injury, retreat and missed attacks. Native `MonsterAI`
owns movement, facing, attack timing, block and execution during committed combat.

Work remaining after traversal/spacing solidification:

1. Common thrown/AOE attack profile: held/stowed object, throw animation,
   projectile/fuse/radius/damage/status, ally filtering, telegraph, ammo and cooldown.
2. First bounded vanilla-derived bomb acceptance scenario with no owner/friendly harm.
3. Heavy-weapon capability and timing through the same role/capability contracts.
4. Classes/roles as data and capability composition, not copied AI stacks.
5. Biome specialists and world-key gating.
6. Hands-off raid/boss observations, failure/recovery and multiplayer proof.

Combat voice 1.0.32.6 remains locked while these systems are added.
