- Crouched-leader concealment remains intact unless the leader, companion, or party is actually attacked.

- Awareness gaze is evaluated before casual ambient/leader gaze; native attack aim remains highest priority.
- Correct formation distance no longer blocks ambient guard behavior as an external action.
- Inventory, merchant, placement, crafting-station, and map use accelerate the ambient handoff; ordinary standing keeps a distinct delay.
- Exposed Human NPCs receive Valheim's native Wet status during rain. Native status duration and stamina-regeneration effects remain authoritative.
