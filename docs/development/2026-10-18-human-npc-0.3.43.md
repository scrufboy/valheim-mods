# Human NPC 0.3.43 atomic combat handoff

Date: 2026-10-18

## Governing rule

Companion behavior and combat are separate ownership domains.

- Peaceful companion behavior covers follow/travel, menus and leader idle, ordinary idle, hold/stay, status presentation, social gaze, awareness/curiosity, torches, healing and ambient activity.
- Awareness may track and visibly indicate a possible threat without entering combat.
- Once a legal encounter is authorized, one `CombatOwnsActor` state becomes the only authority signal used by non-combat systems.
- Native `MonsterAI` owns combat movement, facing, weapon selection, pursuit and attacks.
- Repeated clear scans end combat ownership; only then can the correct saved companion context resume.

## Structural correction

- Removed the parallel per-frame weapon, turn, draw, attack, block and riposte controller.
- Removed custom weapon-selection override and firing-lane arbitration from combat execution.
- Combat authorization now atomically cancels cleric casting and releases the torch before native combat's first update.
- Social gaze, posture, healing, equipment presentation and torch handling now consume the single combat-owner state instead of independently inferring combat from animation, blocking, alert, target and equipment details.
- Peaceful leader weapon readiness can reserve hands without mutating the combat-clear timer.
- Torch return remains gated by the established verified-clear sequence and post-combat delay.

## Retained behavior

- Heightened awareness, visible curiosity and threat marker before commitment.
- Legal target filtering and bounded encounter exit.
- Native Souls of the Land AI values.
- Narrow bow compatibility bridge; native Attack owns ammo, projectile, hit and damage.
- Stowing, formation, doors, wet parity, party creation and saved follow/hold orders.
