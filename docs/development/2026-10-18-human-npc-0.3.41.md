# Human NPC 0.3.41 combat continuity

Date: 2026-10-18

## Evidence

The 0.3.40 field run recorded 83 combat-authorization transitions for the archer and 92 for the cleric. Video and telemetry showed both actors repeatedly leaving valid fights, falling back toward companion behavior, and reacquiring later. The cleric canceled 10 of 11 observed casts during the run.

## Root causes corrected

- Sustained ranged engagement was only refreshing the pursuit clock inside the six-meter close-threat radius. A visible active target farther away therefore expired on an eight-second cycle.
- A temporary equipment, bow-lane, draw, or casting refusal was treated as an invalid combat target. The attack gate erased authorization instead of refusing only that attack attempt.
- Cleric casting checked only for a current authorized target, not encounter readiness. A cast could begin in the short target-transition gap and be canceled immediately.
- Cleric reserve was charged when a cast began, so an interrupted cast spent reserve without delivering a heal.

## 0.3.41 behavior

- A sensed active hostile anywhere inside the established 30-meter awareness envelope renews the current engagement.
- Recent attributed damage still renews close defense inside six meters.
- Temporary attack-execution gates do not erase a valid target.
- Invalid/dead/out-of-bounds targets still drop through the existing fail-closed path.
- Cleric support stays disabled for the complete combat-readiness interval and spends reserve only on successful delivery.
- Existing weapon stowing, torch combat lock, formation leases, doors, wet parity, awareness HUD, party creation, and Souls of the Land AI numbers remain intact.
