# Living World C.O.R.E. chronicle

An append-only public record of how the world, characters, art, and playable
systems develop. This is not a raw commit log. It records decisions that change
what a reader understands about the project.

## 2026-10-27 — The Second Breath becomes the story foundation

Living World established that earlier people reached Valheim before the player.
They live embodied second lives with hunger, work, blood, relationships, and
meaningful death. Odin knows deeds and usefulness but not every human heart.

The Hearthless and Hearthbound became conditions of wandering and belonging,
rather than races or automatic factions.

## 2026-10-27 — Draugr villages become reclamation stories

Village draugr are bound dead, not recruitable villagers awaiting restoration.
Clearing a settlement releases corrupted remains and makes the place safe for
living survivors to return. Reclamation therefore connects wilderness discovery,
companions, homes, work, defense, and memory.

## 2026-10-27 — One actor core, many kinds of people

Friendly, neutral, hostile, corrupted, freed, and regional variants were placed
on one shared NPC architecture. Species, body, sex, and biome no longer determine
morality. Faction and allegiance are authored, persistent choices.

The Eitrherjar Legionary name and identity were restored: an undead war-captain
whose corrupted battlefield purpose outlived every commander. Its old technical
peace with Fulings is not canon.

## 2026-10-27 — Discovery, roster, and permanent stakes

The project separated world people, known people, bonded companions, residents,
and the active expedition party. The default field party is two; the bonded roster
is six.

Campaign companions must be found through persistent world encounters, not spawned
from a catalogue. Release returns a person to meaningful world life.

The Second Breath has no Third Breath. Actual committed companion death is
permanent, with equipment recovery and memorial identity—but mortality remains
safety-gated until traversal and network proof are complete.

## 2026-10-27 — Combat voice reaches an approved baseline

The companion combat-voice module reached approved baseline 1.0.32.6 with matched
male/female semantic pools, locked timing and priority, and reliable death cries.
The voice work demonstrates the project's method: shared behavior, distinct
identity presentation, evidence-backed tests, and no unnecessary duplicate AI.

## 2026-10-27 — Traversal becomes a core promise

Party spacing moved from fixed destinations toward soft regions, personal space,
sidesteps, blocked waits, and deterministic right-of-way while native Valheim
movement remains the executor.

The next mandatory traversal gate is identity-preserving travel through legal
portals and instanced interiors. It must not duplicate, reroll, revive, abandon,
or reset a companion.

## 2026-10-27 — Visual direction

The project committed ensemble key art and a Plains spear-huntress concept. The
art establishes tone and silhouette; it does not falsely promise that the exact
asset already exists in game.

Future visual entries should link the concept, identify its canon/design status,
and state what has or has not been proven at runtime.

## How this chronicle stays current

Add an entry when a decision changes canon, a people or named character, the
player's relationship to the world, visual identity, a major playable promise, or
the accepted status of a core module.

Each entry must distinguish:

- **Canon** — binding world truth;
- **Design direction** — approved target not yet implemented;
- **Runtime proof** — built, installed, and demonstrated behavior;
- **Concept art** — visual intent, not necessarily a shippable model.

Corrections append a new entry and link the superseded claim. History is not
silently rewritten.
