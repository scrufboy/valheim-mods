# Norse lore source notes for Living World C.O.R.E.

Date: 2026-10-27

Purpose: keep attested material, Valheim canon, project invention, and character
belief from collapsing into false “historical accuracy.” This is a design source
map, not a claim that medieval Scandinavian religion was one consistent system.

## Source hierarchy

1. Iron Gate material for Valheim's own fiction.
2. Poetic Edda poems and early skaldic material for attested mythic language.
3. Snorri's *Prose Edda* with awareness of its date and Christian-era framing.
4. Sagas for literary revenant, oath, feud, hospitality, and social patterns—not
   as direct transcripts of pre-Christian belief.
5. Modern scholarship for interpretation; popular summaries only as discovery
   aids leading back to identified sources.

## Terminology cautions

- **Einherjar/einherji:** Odin's chosen battle-dead associated with Valholl; do not
  use as a generic synonym for elite warrior or Fuling ally.
- **Draugr:** literary uses vary; “corporeal revenant” is safer than treating it as
  a single standardized species.
- **Seidr and galdr:** not interchangeable generic spell schools. Any class using
  these terms needs a narrower source and presentation review.
- **Eir:** an attested name associated with healing/medical skill in source
  tradition. A legacy NPC named Eir needs deliberate identity handling.
- **Myrkvidr:** a dark-wood name/poetic association found in Norse legendary
  geography; using it does not establish one universal mythic forest empire.
- **Elf and dwarf:** source categories are complex and inconsistent. Avoid importing
  modern fantasy biology or morality without labeling it Living World canon.

## Writing rule

When a source supports atmosphere but not the exact event, write “inspired by,”
not “according to Norse mythology.” The project's strongest lore will be honest
about where the old material ends and Living World's story begins.
