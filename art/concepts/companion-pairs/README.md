# Companion-pair visual development

These images are concept references for Claude and other Living World C.O.R.E.
contributors. They are not promises that the pictured armor exists as one prefab,
and they do not authorize new NPC roles. Implementation must use available or
separately approved assets and preserve the project boundaries below.

## Canon boundaries

- Companions begin as formidable biome-native or nomadic survivors.
- Design them as complementary pairs, not as a generic five-slot party.
- A useful pair covers both close-quarter danger and a complementary ranged,
  magical, explosive, thrown, or control channel.
- The female Plains Huntress and female Mountain/Black Forest Mystic are
  gender-specific identities.
- Pathfinder/Archer Scouts may be male or female.
- Dwarves are a body/culture/biome family, not one reserved party slot. Their
  likely weapon language emphasizes hammers and axes, although atgeir and shield
  combinations remain candidates to test.
- Jarl, Maiden, and Steward belong to the existing Maypole/totem settlement
  system. Companions are not generic settlement workers.
- Companions may later become an affiliated town's hunter, guard, captain,
  thane, or hero when established systems allow it.

## Pair directions still under design

### Plains

- Female Huntress: spear thrusting, throwing, pursuit, and recovery.
- Male counterpart: not locked. Explore knives, bombs, thrown tools, and other
  repeatable anti-Fuling or anti-shaman pressure.
- Required decisions: Huntress secondary weapon, spear quantity, recovery under
  pressure, and partner behavior during that recovery window.

### Black Forest / Mountains

- Female Mystic: magic-missile pressure, wards, frost/rune control, and support.
- Dwarf counterpart: close-quarter protection and physical control.
- Test hammer, axe, shield, and atgeir configurations against the Mystic's real
  combat gaps before locking the loadout.

### Pathfinder / Archer Scout

- May be male or female.
- Needs a complementary close-quarter partner when enemies breach bow range.
- Sword-and-shield images are loadout exploration, not a final class decision.

### Mistlands

- Requires its own threat and resource analysis.
- A male fire user is a candidate, not a locked character.
- If selected, pair him with a close-quarter survivor rather than another caster.
