LIVING WORLD HUMAN NPC 0.3.53 — SHIELD, CRAFT AND DOOR FOLLOW REPAIR

This archive replaces the prior Human NPC lab candidate. Extract it inside the
profile's BepInEx folder so the active DLL lands at:

plugins\LivingWorldHumanNpc\LivingWorld.HumanNpc.dll

This build preserves the 0.3.52 combat-continuity repair and fixes three
measured integration failures:
- native one-handed weapon selection now pairs an owned shield, so the bounded
  defense component has a real blocker to raise;
- properly stowed back-slot weapons no longer veto peaceful workstation
  animations;
- a directly observed door on the route to the leader retains the real leader
  goal, including while the doorway crossing is committed;
- nearby side doors are still never inferred or selected;
- an authorized living target remains committed instead of being replaced or
  erased by the quarter-second awareness scan;
- native MonsterAI target update, movement, facing, weapon choice and attack
  cadence run during authorized combat;
- committed combat uses a broad encounter boundary, not the tight follow-station
  leash that was repeatedly revoking combat authorization;
- attributed damage renews the encounter without forcing a mid-strike retarget;
- a small human defense component may raise an equipped shield against a visible,
  close attacker already swinging at that companion;
- shield defense does not move, turn, retarget, equip a weapon, launch an attack,
  or manufacture a riposte;
- torch, follow, visible-back stowing, wetness, door and ambient systems remain
  separate from combat ownership.

Offline verification proves the boundaries and package integrity. Bed use is not
implemented in this candidate. The next live run must compare the RRR/Souls of the
Land reference enemies, then confirm native block results, workstation animation,
and leader-route door passage in telemetry.
