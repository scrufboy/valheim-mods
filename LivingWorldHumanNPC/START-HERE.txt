LIVING WORLD HUMAN NPC 0.3.62 — VISIBLE HAIR + BEARD REPAIR

This archive replaces the prior Human NPC lab candidate. Extract it inside the
profile's BepInEx folder so the active DLL lands at:

plugins\LivingWorldHumanNpc\LivingWorld.HumanNpc.dll

0.3.62 fixes the false appearance success exposed by the 2026-10-20 video.
Valheim had allocated the selected Hair14/Beard22 and Hair34 objects, but the
adapter accepted those objects before proving that their renderers were active,
materialized, attached to the human rig, and present in the human LOD. The
adapter now completes Valheim's native visual pass twice: first to construct
the attachments, then to apply their native hair material/property block. It
will not initialize an actor unless the resulting hair and optional beard have
a live renderer on the rig.

This build preserves the 0.3.56 native combat authority, the 0.3.57 fire-team
cohesion repair, and corrects the meaning of native chase distance:
- Valheim measures m_maxChaseDistance from the NPC's original spawn point, not
  from the moving leader, defended formation or current enemy;
- that stale spawn-point fence is disabled; the live 18-metre leader/nearest-
  teammate boundary now exclusively governs companion pursuit;
- awareness at 30 metres does not grant permission to travel 30 metres;
- hostile enemies continue approaching the party while native ranged spacing,
  shield work, charging circulation and the Souls of the Land 8m/6s/8s circle
  values handle local defensive movement;
- no custom movement loop scripts attacks or replaces native pathfinding;
- companions may break formation to fight, but crossing an 18-metre leader or
  living-party-member boundary converts that fight into bounded local defense;
- after that immediate exchange, the companion retires the local target but
  retains party combat ownership and follows back toward the leader armed;
- an owned encounter retains alerted, sensed hostiles through ordinary native
  target handoff gaps, without turning peaceful awareness into an attack;
- live target/damage/alert evidence is shared across companions bound to the
  same owner, so one member cannot enter peaceful behavior while the other is
  still engaged;
- combat ends only after four complete party-clear scans followed by five
  additional uninterrupted clear seconds;
- an already-started cleric heal survives a momentary line-of-sight obstruction,
  while death, ownership, range, full health and immediate personal threat still
  cancel it;
- visual attack cues extend to 14 metres so shield users can answer visible
  ranged attacks aimed at them;
- native MonsterAI now performs its ordinary FindEnemy and UpdateTarget pass;
- Living World validates the selected target after that pass, only rejecting
  allies, tame creatures, unprovoked wildlife reserved for Hunt mode,
  stealth-hold violations and targets outside the companion/order safety envelope;
- no global IsEnemy filter narrows native combat to a pre-authorized target;
- no companion prefix injects or refuses targets before native selection;
- native EquipBestWeapon owns bow-versus-melee choice from the Souls of the Land
  actor-local combat values; the removed role override can no longer restart
  weapon cadence;
- explicit attack orders and defense of the leader still hand a legal target
  directly to native combat;
- torch, follow, visible-back stowing, wetness, doors, posture, sitting and
  workstation behavior remain intact and are locked out by the same combat owner.
- torch eligibility cannot bypass the established peaceful stow transaction;
  a visible torch is valid only with no visible combat item and the sword/shield
  pair still represented on the back;
- fresh hair and beard choices come only from the native Player catalog, not a
  broad search across every installed customization pack;
- a fresh actor is not marked initialized until its chosen facial-hair assets
  have actually attached to the human rig.

Offline verification proves the structural boundary and package integrity. Live
Unity combat remains the acceptance test. Bed use is not implemented in this candidate.
