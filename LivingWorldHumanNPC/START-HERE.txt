LIVING WORLD HUMAN NPC 0.3.55 — ROLE AND GUARD COORDINATION

This archive replaces the prior Human NPC lab candidate. Extract it inside the
profile's BepInEx folder so the active DLL lands at:

plugins\LivingWorldHumanNpc\LivingWorld.HumanNpc.dll

This build preserves the 0.3.54 native sitting repair and corrects three bounded,
measured integration failures:
- an archer now retains the bow at safe range when native combat has a ready
  attack and a clear lane, while a close personal threat selects sword/shield;
- native MonsterAI still owns combat movement, facing, cadence, targeting,
  projectile creation, damage and ammunition use;
- the cleric may use its existing heal during a safe combat window without
  leaving combat ownership; a close personal threat immediately vetoes or
  interrupts the cast;
- temporary guard directions are chosen against every currently occupied buddy
  sector, rather than only the first buddy found;
- guard sectors remain short-lived activity leases, never permanent assignments;
- torch, follow, visible-back stowing, wetness, doors, posture, sitting and
  workstation behavior are unchanged.

Offline verification proves the boundaries and package integrity. Bed use is not
implemented in this candidate. The next live run must confirm sustained ranged
archery, a safe-window cleric cast, and non-overlapping guard coverage in telemetry.
